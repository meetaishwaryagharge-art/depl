About us

A trading house, and the company behind VMAL.

Dhanisha Enterprises Private Limited trades, imports, exports and distributes goods across many industries, and makes household hygiene products under its own brand, VMAL.

Who we are

Registered in Maharashtra, working across industries.

Dhanisha Enterprises Private Limited is registered in Maharashtra. It trades in goods both manufactured and unmanufactured, wholesale or retail, directly or through agents and distributors.

The company also buys, leases and exchanges property and assets, and evaluates business opportunities that support steady, long-term growth.

Our brand

VMAL Home Care Products

Clean Homes | Healthy Lives.

VMAL is a premium unit of Dhanisha Enterprises, a manufacturer of premium household hygiene solutions, from floor protectants to antibacterial hand wash.

Production uses water treated by a multi-stage Reverse Osmosis purification system, which keeps cleaning agents stable, improves grease removal and lather, and extends shelf life. Premium essential oils give long-lasting fragrance, and the formulations are effective on stains and germs while safe for families and surfaces.

VMAL serves homemakers looking for premium home care, and retail entrepreneurs looking for a distribution partnership.
